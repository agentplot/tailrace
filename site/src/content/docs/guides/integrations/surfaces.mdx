A surface is where a person or a program asks tailrace a question. Every
surface answers from one core, and the same question on any surface gets
the same answer (56). No surface computes a verdict, a version or a
channel of its own.

## The contract

A surface is not a seam of Part B; it is bound by Part A's rules for
askers (56 to 60). Every answer is readable by a program on request, in
a form that names digests, versions, channels and verdicts as the
statement names them (57). tailrace never answers a refusal as an
absence, an absence as a refusal, or either as unknown; a refusal names
the first claim found wanting and why, and carries the underlying
store's, signer's or ecosystem's own words verbatim beside tailrace's
(58, 59). Asking what tailrace holds reaches no store, no signer and no
target, and writes nothing (60).

## Adding one

A surface calls the one core and renders its answer; it reads no store
itself and derives nothing from a tag listing. A surface in the
repository is a moon project of its own, held to the same gate as the
rest; the conformance suite holds the core it calls (93).
