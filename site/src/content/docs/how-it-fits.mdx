tailrace is one of a family of tools that turn what happens outside a
repository into work that lands in it. Each tool stands alone, and each
one's site is its own.

<Family highlight="tailrace" />

## After the landing

The family runs as a mill does. headwater and eddy notice what happens
upstream; millpond keeps it as an inbox; flume carries each item to one
agent in one worktree; the agents work; sluice tests each change as merged
and lands it. tailrace is the channel that carries the water away after
it has done its work: what sluice landed goes out to the environments as
signed artifacts, and the family diagram above ends where tailrace begins.

tailrace reads two things the family already makes: sluice's refs, which
say which commit landed and what its checks said, and moon's task hashes,
which say what the build left. Neither sluice nor moon knows tailrace
exists. The verdicts sluice signed for the landed commit become an
attestation on each artifact the landing produced, so a consumer can read
at the registry what the merge queue decided.

## Alone, or as a layer

tailrace needs none of the others. Any repository whose build leaves
artifacts can push them with their provenance, and any registry with the
referrers API, or a directory on disk, can hold them. Publishing stands
alone; channels and environments are layers on it, each optional, so a
platform that keeps its own channels and promotion takes tailrace's
publishing and leaves the rest. sluice and tailrace make a delivery pair:
one decides what lands, the other what runs, and the digest passes between
them with its proof attached.

## A thin center

What the family centralizes is small: identity, policy and a signed
ledger. Builds, tests and packaging run where the agents run, on
developer machines and agent hosts, and the artifacts stay in the user's
own registry. The direction is a hosted trust plane later, a private
certificate authority tied to the user's identity provider, a transparency
log, promotion policy and countersigning, that holds none of the
artifacts and none of the ledger; both stay in the user's account.
