---
title: Roadmap
description: The steps that build tailrace, in order, and what each one is done by.
---

The statement is being written, and nothing is built. These steps build
tailrace in the order the design pack proposes, the first crate arriving
with the first ADR. Each is done by a check or an observation, never by a
list ticked by hand. Nothing hosted is built until people running the
open-source tool ask for it.

**T1 · Write the statement.** tailrace's invariants as numbered clauses in
agentplot/blueprints, with the operator's rulings on the questions the
design pack leaves open: the policy language, the promotion record's
schema, the OCI conventions, and where a local-first store keeps its
blobs.\
*Needs* the operator's rulings. *Done when* Vale is clean and the operator
agrees to land it on main.

**T2 · Scaffold the crate.** The founding decisions as ADRs, with the
rules in `docs/architecture/core.md`: a Rust binary with seams for the
registry, the signer and the targets; `.config/tailrace.toml`; devenv,
moon and sluice's gate.\
*Needs* T1. *Done when* sluice protects main and the first change lands
through it.

**T3 · Push, by digest.** `tailrace push` packages what the build left,
signs it, and pushes it into quarantine in a file-based OCI layout on disk
or ECR, with the provenance and sluice's verdicts as referrers and a
machine-readable answer; `tailrace publish` as the smallest gate, copying
the same digest into the artifacts and pointing `beta` at it.\
*Needs* T2. *Done when* an artifact pushed from a landing on one of the
family's own repositories verifies, with its referrers, from a second
machine.

**T4 · The cache.** moon's remote cache backed by the cache namespace of
the same store.\
*Needs* T3. *Done when* a task that passed on one host replays from the
store on another.

**T5 · Channels, by proof.** `beta` and `stable` from the command line;
`tailrace promote` writes a signed record to an append-only ledger, and
`tailrace status` reads it.\
*Needs* T3. *Done when* a promotion made on one machine is read on
another, and a rollback is a promotion to an older version.

**T6 · Sync and serve.** `tailrace sync` fills npm's local layout from the
store under a policy, then the other ecosystems; `tailrace serve` answers
npm's read protocol on localhost.\
*Needs* T5. *Done when* `npm install` in a repository resolves a package
only from a version whose attestations satisfy the policy, and a version
that fails it is refused with the reason.

**T7 · The Nix spike.** A Nix binary cache over the store, measured
against Attic on one closure.\
*Needs* T4. *Done when* the measurement says whether tailrace serves store
paths itself or layers on Attic, and the statement records it.

**T8 · Environments.** A target, a channel, an `auto` or `manual` policy
and a reconciler, push or pull; Flux and Argo consuming the channel tags
with a status hook; reconcilers of tailrace's own for CloudFormation,
Lambda, static sites and Clan.\
*Needs* T5. *Done when* a `stable` promotion converges a staging
environment under `auto`, and production waits under `manual` until
approved.

**T9 · Verified releases.** The independent rebuild with the shared cache
off, its matching digest as an attestation, and two signatures before
`stable` moves; a reference self-hosted Sigstore.\
*Needs* T8. *Done when* a `stable` promotion is refused without the second
signature, and accepted with it.

## After tailrace

The formats and the command line freeze at 1.0. A hosted trust plane, a
private certificate authority tied to the user's identity provider, a
transparency log, promotion policy and countersigning, comes only when
people running the open-source tool ask for it, and holds none of their
artifacts and none of their ledger.
